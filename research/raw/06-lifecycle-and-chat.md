# Listing Lifecycle & Buyer Chat

Research date: 2026-08-27. Scope: Facebook Marketplace listing lifecycle (post-publish) and buyer-chat mechanics, as documented by Meta.

Methodology note: Meta's help pages (facebook.com/help, facebook.com/business/help) are heavily JS-rendered and geo/locale-gated. Automated fetches in this session resolved to a Philippines locale, returning body text in Filipino/Tagalog (translations provided below, with original quoted where useful) and, for several commerce features (shipping, checkout, Purchase Protection, boosted listings), a "not available in your location" notice instead of substantive content. This itself is a documented finding — those features are region-gated and Meta shows different (or no) documentation depending on where the requesting account/browser is located. Where I could not retrieve substantive body text despite finding the correct official URL, this is flagged explicitly rather than papered over with inference.

## LIFECYCLE

### Does a listing expire? What does Meta officially say about duration?

- Meta's own "Selling on Marketplace" and "Sell something on Facebook Marketplace" help pages (facebook.com/help/153832041692242, facebook.com/help/561376580709359) list the steps to create a listing (add photos, enter details, publish) and post-listing actions (edit, mark as sold) but **do not state a fixed expiration period, a "days active" number, or an explicit decay/ranking policy** in the content retrievable via this session. [META-OFFICIAL, but incomplete/inconclusive — https://www.facebook.com/help/153832041692242, https://www.facebook.com/help/561376580709359]
- No official Meta page was found (in this research pass) that states a specific number of days (e.g., "7 days" or "30 days") after which a listing automatically expires. **This appears to be an undocumented (or at least not publicly, precisely documented) mechanic.**
- Third-party/community sources claim specific durations — most commonly "7 days" until a listing needs renewal, or "30 days" as a rough visibility lifespan — but these numbers vary between sources and are not sourced to any Meta document. [SECONDARY/UNVERIFIED — e.g. https://carsnipe.com/blog/how-long-facebook-marketplace-listings-last ("7 Days"); https://savemyleads.com/blog/other/how-long-do-ads-stay-on-facebook-marketplace; general consensus paraphrase: "listing stays live until sold/deleted/removed but visibility decays after the first week" — UNVERIFIED COMMUNITY LORE, no Meta citation found]
- **Gap:** Meta does not appear to publish a precise, citable "listings expire after X days" policy. Treat any specific day-count as community lore unless a primary source surfaces.

### What EXACTLY does "Renew listing" do? Waiting period? Renewal limits?

- **No dedicated official Meta help article documenting the "Renew listing" button was found**, despite targeted searches for `facebook.com/help "renew" listing marketplace`. The closest official touchpoints are the "Edit your Facebook Marketplace listing" page (facebook.com/help/514314075439323) and the "What to do if your Facebook Marketplace listing is rejected" page (facebook.com/help/2193854224216494), neither of which documents renew mechanics directly. [META-OFFICIAL page exists but does not cover renew — https://www.facebook.com/help/514314075439323]
- The rejected-listing page does state that Meta will not restore/allow further action on listings removed for Commerce Policy or Community Standards violations, which — per secondary sources — is also why such listings can't be renewed: "Posible naming i-reject ang iyong listing kung makita naming hindi ito sumusunod sa aming Mga Commerce Policy o Mga Pamantayan ng Komunidad" (roughly: "We may reject your listing if we find it doesn't comply with our Commerce Policies or Community Standards"). [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/2193854224216494]
- Community/secondary sources describe: a waiting period before renewal becomes available (commonly cited as ~7 days), renewal being free, renewal "bumping" the listing to the top of search/recency without needing to re-enter details, and a cap on total renewal cycles (e.g., "roughly 5 renewal cycles" before the option greys out) — **none of this is sourced to a Meta document** and should be treated as unverified. [UNVERIFIED COMMUNITY LORE — https://www.gmapswidget.com/why-cant-i-renew-my-listing-on-facebook-marketplace-solutions-explained/, https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/what-does-renew-listing-mean-on-facebook-marketplace, https://socialoapp.com/blog/what-does-renew-listing-mean-on-facebook-marketplace/]
- **Gap:** This is one of the least-documented areas of Marketplace. Meta does not appear to publish the renewal cadence, limits, or exact mechanical effect (e.g., whether it resets the "listed date," whether it clears saves/messages) anywhere publicly findable. One secondary claim states renewal "recreates the listing and discards every save, share and message" — this is a strong, specific, unverified claim that would be important if true, but has no Meta citation. [UNVERIFIED COMMUNITY LORE]

### Documented differences: renew vs. edit vs. mark as pending vs. mark as sold vs. hide/delete vs. relist

- **Edit**: The "Edit your Facebook Marketplace listing" page confirms sellers can edit listing details ("Pag-edit ng mga detalye ng iyong listing"), and separately can "mark listings as sold or pending" ("Pagmarka sa iyong listing bilang nabenta na o pending") or delete them, from the same management surface. It also notes multi-item listings automatically show remaining inventory count without needing an edit, once 10 or fewer items remain. [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/514314075439323]
- **Mark as sold**: The dedicated help page "Mark an item as sold on Facebook Marketplace" documents concrete effects:
  - The listing "will no longer be visible to others on Marketplace" ("Hindi na ito makikita ng ibang tao sa Marketplace").
  - "All interested buyers [who messaged about the item] receive a notification that the item is sold" ("Ang lahat ng buyer na nag-message sa iyo tungkol sa item ay makakakuha ng message na sinasabi na naibenta na ang item").
  - The seller is prompted to archive the conversation thread tied to that listing.
  - The same page notes other available statuses beyond "Sold": Pending, Available, Shipped. [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/1680504982210398]
- **Mark as pending**: No dedicated Meta help article specifically documenting "Mark as Pending" was found (searched explicitly; only "Mark an item as sold" exists as a standalone doc, which mentions "Pending" as one of the available statuses in passing). Community sources describe pending as: the listing stays visible to other buyers but signals a transaction is in progress; the seller can revert to "Available" if the deal falls through; buyers who already messaged may be notified of the status. **This buyer-visibility behavior is not confirmed by a dedicated Meta document** — treat it as secondary. [SECONDARY/UNVERIFIED — https://www.itgeared.com/what-does-pending-mean-on-facebook-marketplace/, https://onaircode.com/pending-mean-on-facebook-marketplace/, https://www.techwiseguide.com/post/how-to-manage-and-remove-pending-status-on-facebook-marketplace]
- **Hide/Delete**: Referenced only in passing on the edit-listing help page as an available action ("delete listings entirely"); no separate Meta document with detail was found in this pass. [META-OFFICIAL, thin — https://www.facebook.com/help/514314075439323]
- **Relist / "List again"**: No official Meta documentation of a distinct "relist" mechanic (as opposed to renew, or manually creating a new listing) was found. Community guidance treats "delete and repost from scratch" as the fallback once renewal is unavailable, but this is not an officially named/documented feature. [UNVERIFIED COMMUNITY LORE]
- **Gap summary**: Meta documents "edit," "mark as sold," and (in passing) "pending"/"delete" as listing-management actions, with mark-as-sold being the only one with clearly documented downstream effects (visibility removal + buyer notification). Renew and relist are essentially undocumented publicly by Meta.

### Effect of editing (especially price) on recency/visibility; price-drop notifications

- The retrievable content of the official "Edit your listing" page does not mention any effect on ranking, recency, or visibility from editing. [META-OFFICIAL, silent on this point — https://www.facebook.com/help/514314075439323]
- Meta does document, elsewhere in its consumer-facing product behavior (not confirmed via a fetchable help-center article in this session, only via search-result synthesis), that lowering a listed price triggers a notification to people who saved the item. This is a widely repeated claim but I could not pull the exact wording of a Meta help article stating it directly — flagging as **SECONDARY**, likely true given it matches well-documented "Saved items" notification patterns elsewhere on Facebook/Instagram Marketplace-adjacent products, but not confirmed against primary text in this pass. [SECONDARY — search-synthesized, no primary Meta URL retrieved with this exact claim]
- **Gap:** No official Meta statement was found either confirming or denying that editing price "bumps" a listing's recency/ranking. Community folklore that price edits (or any edit) refresh a listing's position is common but unverified.

### "Mark as Pending" — visibility to buyers

- Covered above: no dedicated Meta doc found. Best-available claim (buyers can still see item, understand a transaction is in progress, can still message/save) is **SECONDARY/UNVERIFIED**, not confirmed by Meta.

### Same item in multiple categories; Marketplace + Buy/Sell Groups cross-posting; group cross-posting limits

- **Multiple categories**: No official Meta documentation states a listing can belong to more than one category. Secondary guidance is consistent that a single listing has one category, and that reaching multiple categories requires separate listings; category can be changed after posting via Edit. [SECONDARY — https://onaircode.com/select-a-category-on-facebook-marketplace/, general search synthesis; no primary Meta text located on this exact point]
- **Buy/Sell Groups**: Meta's official "Edit your listing in a Facebook buy and sell group" page confirms buy-and-sell-group posts are edited from the Group itself (Groups → the buy/sell group → your post → Edit post), covering price, images, and information. It documents an age requirement (18+ in the US where applicable) and that the feature is "only available in select groups and countries." **It does not document any automatic sync/cross-post relationship between a Marketplace listing and a group post** — they read as governed by separate mechanisms in Meta's own documentation, with only a "related article" pointer to the general Marketplace-listing edit page rather than an explanation of how the two interact. [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/678743755557904]
- The Marketplace help-overview page (facebook.com/help/1127970530677256) does not list a group cross-posting explainer among its indexed subtopics (account status, rejected listings, prohibited items, reporting sellers, blocking). [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/1127970530677256]
- **Gap:** Meta does not appear to publicly document a formal limit on cross-posting the same item across multiple Buy/Sell Groups, or a rate limit for how fast this can be done. Secondary/community claims that posting "too fast" or into "formally linked" groups can trip spam detection, and that Facebook has an official group-linking feature for controlled cross-posting, are plausible but not sourced to a specific Meta help article in this pass. [UNVERIFIED COMMUNITY LORE — https://www.lilachbullock.com/facebook-marketplace-groups-sell-more/ and similar]

### Limits on active listings / listings per day

- Meta's own page, "Facebook Marketplace listing limits" (facebook.com/help/811082570742714), does exist and does state a **country-specific numeric limit** — in the Philippines-localized version retrieved, it states: "Sa Pilipinas, pwede kang mag-post ng hanggang 10 listing kada linggo sa Facebook Marketplace" ("In the Philippines, you can post up to 10 listings per week on Facebook Marketplace"). This confirms the page's structure is **country-specific**, meaning the limit for other regions (e.g., US) would appear on the same URL but render a different number depending on the requester's location — this session could not retrieve the US/English figure. [META-OFFICIAL, confirmed structure, region-specific numeric content not confirmed for all regions — https://www.facebook.com/help/811082570742714]
- No total "active listings at once" cap was found documented by Meta in the retrievable content; the only concrete number found is the per-week posting cap (region-dependent).
- Secondary sources cite figures like "up to 150 new listings per day" for general accounts, "10 vehicles per day" for auto listings, and "1 per day" as a common new-account starting limit that scales up with account history — **none of these numbers are confirmed against Meta's own page in this session** and should be treated as unverified/approximate. [UNVERIFIED COMMUNITY LORE — https://savemyleads.com/blog/other/how-many-ads-can-you-post-on-facebook-marketplace, https://www.carvidapp.com/facebook-marketplace-dealer-posting-rules/, https://socialthink.io/blog/temporary-limit-on-facebook-marketplace-new-accounts/]

### Seller-side stats per listing (views, saves, messages, shares) — Insights

- Meta documents a Marketplace **Insights** feature via two official pages: "Insights on Facebook Marketplace" (facebook.com/help/937527940168419) and "Access Insights on Facebook Marketplace" (facebook.com/help/265775098821464).
- Documented metrics (per the Insights overview page): number of times listings were **clicked**, number of times listings were **saved**, number of times listings were **shared**, and number of people **following the seller's Marketplace profile**. (Note: "messages" as a discrete counted metric was not explicitly listed among these four in the retrieved text — clicks/saves/shares/followers were the four named.) [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/937527940168419]
- Access path (per the "Access Insights" page): Feed → Marketplace → **Selling** → **Seller Dashboard** for insights across all listings; Feed → Marketplace → Selling → **Your listings** → select a specific listing, for per-listing insights.
- Insights can be filtered by **last 7 days, last 14 days, or last 30 days**.
- Both pages carry the same notice: "your shipping performance and history may affect your listing distribution on Marketplace" ("Ang iyong shipping performance at history ay maaaring maapektuhan ang pamamahagi ng iyong mga listing sa Marketplace") — i.e., Meta officially documents that shipping performance (for sellers using Marketplace shipping) is a factor in how listings get distributed/surfaced. [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/937527940168419, https://www.facebook.com/help/265775098821464]

## BUYER CHAT

### How do buyer messages arrive — Messenger or a separate Marketplace inbox?

- Meta documents **both**: a dedicated Marketplace **Inbox** and Messenger both surface the same conversations. Per "View your Facebook Marketplace messages" (facebook.com/help/347147066002616): click the Marketplace icon → **Inbox** in the left menu to see Marketplace conversations, filterable by transaction status (e.g., pending offers, paid, shipped). The same page explicitly states: "you can also view messages with buyers and sellers in Messenger" ("Pwede mo ring tingnan ang iyong mga message kasama ng mga buyer at seller sa Messenger"). Sellers can also group chats by listing ("Individual chats" grouping). Note: a conversation can disappear from default views if it was archived at the time its listing was marked sold. [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/347147066002616]
- There is also a page "About Marketplace messages" (facebook.com/help/1166142028428322) that exists in Meta's help index but returned no substantive body content in this session beyond linking to related articles (viewing messages, "can't send messages," Meta's review of messages for suspicious activity, stolen-item guidance, "buying and selling confidently"). [META-OFFICIAL, page exists, content not retrievable in this pass]

### "Is this still available?" prefilled/quick-reply message

- Confirmed via Messenger's official help page, "Buy something on Facebook Marketplace" (messenger.com/help/1723922281209915): buyers have two ways to contact a seller — click **Message** to send a custom message, or click **Send** to send a quick pre-written message asking "Is this available?" ("I-click ang Ipadala para magpadala ng message na sinasabing 'Available ba ito?'"). The same page notes this Messenger-based purchase flow "may be unavailable in Japan and for countries located in Europe." [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.messenger.com/help/1723922281209915]

### Saved replies / automated responses / away messages — available to individual sellers?

- Meta documents **"Saved replies"** as a Messenger/Page feature: a Page admin can save a frequently-used response and reuse it; saved replies are also proactively suggested when relevant to the current conversation; replies support auto-populating placeholders (person's first/last name, admin's name, website URL). This documentation is framed around **Pages** (via "Inbox Suggestions" in Meta Business Suite and the "Messaging Replies" business help article), not personal profiles. [META-OFFICIAL, page-level framing — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1803093376646651 ("Messaging Replies"), https://www.facebook.com/business/help/991413128263758 ("About Inbox Suggestions in Facebook Business Suite") — note: full body text of these two Business Suite pages was not retrievable in this session beyond titles/summaries already known from search snippets]
- **Individual/personal-profile Marketplace sellers** (i.e., a person selling as themselves, not as a Page/Business) are not documented as having a "saved replies" or automated away-message feature scoped to Marketplace specifically. Meta's saved-replies tooling is consistently described in the context of Pages / Business Suite, not personal Marketplace selling. [Inference from META-OFFICIAL page scoping — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1803093376646651; absence of any personal-profile equivalent found]
- **Gap:** No Meta document was found stating "personal Marketplace sellers do NOT have saved replies" explicitly (an absence, not a confirmed prohibition) — but no evidence of the feature being extended to personal-profile Marketplace chat was found either.

### "Response rate" / "Very responsive" indicator — calculation and display

- **Two distinct but related documented mechanics** were found, one for Pages/Messenger generally, one for Marketplace sellers specifically:
  1. **Pages — "Very responsive to messages" badge** (general Messenger/Page feature, not Marketplace-specific): per "Learn how Facebook Pages get the 'Very responsive to messages' badge" (facebook.com/help/475643069256244), a Page earns this badge by meeting, within the past 7 days: a **90% response rate** and a **response time under 15 minutes**. The badge becomes publicly visible once earned; without it, only Page managers can see the underlying response metrics. There is also a corresponding Meta Business Help Center article, "About the Very Responsive Badge for Facebook Messenger" (facebook.com/business/help/201893553741970), whose full body text was not retrievable in this session (title/existence confirmed only). [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/475643069256244; existence-only — https://www.facebook.com/business/help/201893553741970]
  2. **Marketplace — seller badges**, documented on "Get seller badges on Facebook" (facebook.com/help/1684084458520855): Meta names (Philippines-localized) six seller badges, including one translated as **"Very Responsive"** ("Talagang Sumasagot"), alongside "Active Local Seller," "Highly Rated," "Super Seller," and "Top Shipper." The page states badges "help the community identify sellers with a good history of selling on Facebook" and that badges appear on the seller's Marketplace profile, visible to Marketplace users. **The exact calculation/threshold for the Marketplace-specific "Very Responsive" badge (as opposed to the Page badge's 90%/15-min thresholds) was not present in the retrievable content** — the page has "How do I get [badge]?" section headers whose answer text did not render in this fetch. [META-OFFICIAL, thresholds not confirmed — https://www.facebook.com/help/1684084458520855]
- **Gap:** It is not confirmed from primary sources whether the Marketplace seller "Very Responsive" badge uses the same 90%-response-rate/15-minute thresholds documented for Pages, or a separate Marketplace-specific formula. Secondary sources describe a Marketplace-specific "Fast Replier" concept (distinct name) requiring ~80% of messages answered within roughly an hour, but this is **not sourced to a Meta document** and conflicts somewhat with the "Very Responsive" badge name found on the official seller-badges page — treat both the numeric thresholds and the "Fast Replier" name as unverified. [UNVERIFIED COMMUNITY LORE — https://www.edesk.com/blog/facebook-very-responsive-to-messages-badge/, https://tools.oneshop.com/blog/facebook-marketplace-badges]

### Blocking/reporting a buyer; rating/feedback system

- **Reporting a buyer**: Documented via Messenger's "Report a Facebook Marketplace buyer" (messenger.com/help/280774317577363). Path: Feed → Marketplace → Selling → Seller Dashboard → Your Listings → open the listing the buyer purchased/messaged about → open the message thread → **Options** → **Report the Buyer** → follow on-screen instructions. The page does not document specific consequences of a report (no detail on outcomes, review process, or buyer-side effects). [META-OFFICIAL, process documented, outcomes not documented — https://www.messenger.com/help/280774317577363]
- **Blocking**: The Marketplace help-overview page lists "Block a person on Facebook Marketplace" as an indexed help topic, but its own body content was not retrieved in this pass. [META-OFFICIAL, existence only — https://www.facebook.com/help/1127970530677256]
- **Rating/feedback system**: Confirmed as an official, documented Marketplace feature, with several distinct pages:
  - "How ratings work on Facebook Marketplace" (facebook.com/help/915385548593204): buyer and seller ratings can **only be given from the Facebook app for iPhone or Android** — not from facebook.com or Facebook Lite ("Pwedeng lang ibigay ang mga rating ng buyer at seller mula sa Facebook app para sa iPhone o Android, hindi mula sa facebook.com o Facebook Lite"). **Buyer ratings are private** ("Pribado ang mga rating sa buyer"). **Seller ratings become public only after the seller has received 5 or more ratings** ("Magigiging naka-public ang mga rating sa seller pagkatapos mong makatanggap ng 5 o higit pang rating"). Ratings (buyer-side viewing) are only visible in the Facebook mobile app for iPhone/Android. Meta explicitly disclaims verification: "We do not verify whether a user actually purchased the product or used the service they're rating/recommending on Marketplace." Purpose stated as sharing feedback to help others on Marketplace. [META-OFFICIAL — https://www.facebook.com/help/915385548593204]
  - "How seller reviews work on Facebook Marketplace" (facebook.com/help/2976188062670725): confirms seller reviews exist as a feature (available on mobile app, not on desktop/computer per this page), with related pages covering reporting a review, replying to a review, and viewing profiles/ratings — but this page's own substantive body text (who can review, whether star-based, etc.) was not retrievable in this pass. Search-snippet-level synthesis (not primary-quoted) adds: reviews are posted as text comments visible on a seller's Marketplace profile and on the specific listing; once public, a review can't be edited (but can be deleted by its author); a seller can reply once, and the reviewer cannot reply again to the seller's reply; sellers can request a review from a buyer who hasn't left one. [META-OFFICIAL page confirmed to exist; most mechanic detail is SECONDARY/search-synthesized — https://www.facebook.com/help/2976188062670725; secondary corroboration only, no further primary URL retrieved]
  - "Rate a Facebook Marketplace seller" (facebook.com/help/1049892879215022) and "Request ratings from buyers on Facebook Marketplace" (facebook.com/help/757344028516600) both exist officially; neither page's rating-mechanic detail (stars vs. thumbs vs. tags) was retrievable in this session. **Gap: it is not confirmed from primary text whether Marketplace ratings are a star scale, thumbs up/down, or tag-based** — this remains undocumented in what could be retrieved. [META-OFFICIAL, existence only — https://www.facebook.com/help/1049892879215022, https://www.facebook.com/help/757344028516600]
  - **Visibility on listings**: Not confirmed directly from primary text whether individual listing pages surface the seller's rating/badge, versus only the seller's Marketplace profile page. The seller-badges page states badges show "on the seller's Marketplace profile," which is distinct from the listing page itself; no primary confirmation either way for whether ratings/badges also render inline on listing cards. [Gap]

### Marketplace checkout/shipping vs. local pickup — payment protection, fees, payout timing

- This entire area is **heavily region-gated**, and Meta's own pages state this outright. Every official page fetched in this pass ("Manage items sold with delivery/shipping" facebook.com/help/2821302044815938, "Get paid for selling with shipping on Marketplace" facebook.com/help/449101635835192, "About shipping on Facebook Marketplace" facebook.com/help/796066857221106, "How Purchase Protection works on Facebook" facebook.com/help/228307904608701) returned a **"not currently available in your location"** notice as their substantive content in the Philippines-localized fetch used in this session, rather than the underlying policy text. This is itself a documented fact: Meta gates shipping/checkout and Purchase Protection by region and shows different (or no) detail depending on the account's market. [META-OFFICIAL, confirms regional gating — https://www.facebook.com/help/2821302044815938, https://www.facebook.com/help/449101635835192, https://www.facebook.com/help/796066857221106, https://www.facebook.com/help/228307904608701]
- From secondary/search-snippet synthesis (not confirmed against primary body text retrieved in this session, but attributed by the search tool to Facebook's own Purchase Protection materials): Purchase Protection is described as free and automatic for eligible orders completed via Facebook's onsite checkout with shipping; it covers non-receipt, damaged/misdescribed items, seller refund-policy violations, and unauthorized purchases; approved claims refund the full purchase price plus shipping; local pickup, Messenger-only transactions, and third-party-site purchases are explicitly excluded from coverage; the dispute flow requires the buyer to contact the seller within 3 days of delivery, the seller then has 2 days to respond, after which the buyer can escalate a claim to Meta directly; payouts to sellers are described as releasing to the seller's linked account after the buyer receives the item and a protection/dispute window passes (not immediately at purchase). **These specific eligibility rules, timeframes (3 days / 2 days), and payout-timing description could not be re-confirmed against the primary Meta page text in this session** (the page returned a location-gated notice instead) — they are presented here as SECONDARY pending direct primary confirmation, though they read as plausible restatements of Meta's own policy given their specificity and internal consistency. [SECONDARY, pending primary re-confirmation — synthesized from search results referencing facebook.com/legal/merchant_policies and facebook.com/help/228307904608701, not independently verified against primary body text in this pass]
- **Local pickup** is documented, in the retrievable page fragments, as the fallback/default path when shipping/checkout isn't available in a seller's location ("sell something locally on Marketplace"), but no dedicated primary-sourced comparison table of "local pickup vs. shipping checkout" protections was retrievable. ## Confidence & Gaps

**High confidence (primary text directly retrieved and quoted):**
- Mark-as-sold effects: listing hidden from Marketplace, interested buyers notified, archive prompt shown. (facebook.com/help/1680504982210398)
- Marketplace Insights metrics (clicks, saves, shares, followers) and access path (Seller Dashboard / Your Listings), filterable by 7/14/30 days; shipping performance affects listing distribution. (facebook.com/help/937527940168419, /265775098821464)
- Marketplace buyer-messaging entry points: dedicated Marketplace Inbox + Messenger both surface the same conversations; quick "Is this available?" message vs. custom message. (facebook.com/help/347147066002616, messenger.com/help/1723922281209915)
- Page-level "Very responsive to messages" badge thresholds: 90% response rate, <15 min response time, trailing 7 days. (facebook.com/help/475643069256244)
- Marketplace ratings: mobile-app-only, buyer ratings private, seller ratings public only after 5+ ratings, no purchase verification. (facebook.com/help/915385548593204)
- Reporting a buyer: documented UI path via Seller Dashboard → listing → message thread → Options → Report. (messenger.com/help/280774317577363)
- Regional gating of shipping/checkout/Purchase Protection is real and Meta's own pages display "not available in your location" rather than policy text outside supported markets.

**Low confidence / undocumented by Meta (treat with caution, largely community lore):**
- Exact listing lifespan/expiration period (no Meta-stated day-count found).
- "Renew listing" mechanics: waiting period, renewal cap/count, whether it clears saves/messages, whether it resets recency — no dedicated Meta article found at all.
- "Mark as Pending" buyer-visibility behavior — no dedicated Meta article found; only inferred from the mark-as-sold page's passing mention that Pending is one of several statuses.
- Whether editing (especially price) affects ranking/recency — not addressed in retrievable Meta text either way.
- Price-drop notifications to people who saved an item — plausible, widely repeated, but a directly-quotable primary source was not retrieved in this session.
- Numeric limits on active listings / listings per day outside the one confirmed region-specific example (Philippines: 10/week) — the page structure is confirmed to be region-specific and official, but figures for other regions (notably the US) were not retrieved.
- Marketplace-specific "Very Responsive" badge's exact calculation (vs. the confirmed Page-level 90%/15-min formula) — the badge's existence and name are confirmed officially, but its own threshold text did not render.
- Cross-posting limits between Marketplace and Buy/Sell Groups, and between multiple Buy/Sell Groups — no rate limit or cap documented by Meta; group-linking and spam-filter claims are community lore.
- Whether ratings use a star scale, thumbs, or tags — not confirmed from primary text.
- Purchase Protection specific timeframes (3-day/2-day dispute windows) and payout-timing description — read as plausible and internally consistent but not re-confirmed against primary body text in this session due to regional gating; labeled SECONDARY pending direct verification from a non-gated locale/account.

**Methodological caveat:** Several officially-existing Meta help URLs were confirmed (correct title, correct topic) but their full body text did not render through the fetch tool in this session — likely due to heavy client-side rendering and/or locale/geo gating. Where this occurred, it is flagged inline as "existence-only" or "page exists, content not retrievable" rather than treated as a documented absence. A follow-up pass using a US/English-locale authenticated session (or Meta's official mobile app) would likely surface additional primary text, particularly for: renew mechanics, mark-as-pending, the Marketplace-specific Very Responsive badge formula, rating-scale mechanics, and the Purchase Protection claims process.
